// 001 — Initiative

Where Science Meets the Deep Blue

OceanLab is a multidisciplinary ocean research programme focused on understanding the biological, chemical, and physical dynamics of deep-sea ecosystems.

Our experiments operate at depths ranging from 200 m to 4,000 m, deploying autonomous drones, chemical sensors, and spectral cameras to capture data previously unreachable by conventional means.

// 003 — Results

Key Findings

01

Bioluminescent Species Density

76% of organisms sampled below 1,000 m demonstrate some form of light production — far exceeding prior estimates from surface surveys.

02

Novel Chemosynthetic Pathway

Identified a previously undocumented sulphur-oxidising pathway in vent bacteria capable of sustaining complex food webs without solar energy.

03

Micro-Plastic Penetration

Micro-plastics detected in sediment samples at 4,100 m — the deepest confirmed penetration recorded in this ocean basin.

04

Thermohaline Acceleration

Deep circulation currents running 12% faster than baseline data from 2010, consistent with modelled climate disruption scenarios.
